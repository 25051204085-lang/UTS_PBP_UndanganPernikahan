Our Story

Our story began at Wuthering Heights, where fate brought together a young lady from a grand estate and a youth from the harsh city streets. Amidst the chill of the manor, the old hearth stood as a silent witness to laughter over simple riddles, endless conversations, and the quiet courage to protect one another—weaving a thread of fate that would test time, distance, and destiny. Across its rolling hills, we found the violet heath, a resilient bloom that retains its color despite bitter winds and barren moors. From this flower, a ring was woven: a symbol of endurance and a constant reminder that the warmth of our love would never fade beneath any storm.

Yet, our journey was never easy. Misunderstandings, painful silences, and a long separation once carved a deep rift between us. Wandering through countless possibilities and shadows of the past, we ultimately realized one absolute truth: there is no other refuge for our hearts than by each other's side. After finding the courage to break down the walls of silence and speak every honest whisper of our hearts, we have finally come home. Today, we close the chapter of grief to carve out a bright new destiny, stepping forward together with the violet heath toward the altar to unite two souls that have found their way back to one another.
